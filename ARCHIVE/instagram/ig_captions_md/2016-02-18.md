People often ask us about soups. Why are there no soups in recommendations for nutrition? Are they not allowed?
Of course they are allowed! Soup can become a great meal. Thus it is better to pay attention to the nutritional value of the portion - soup should have some protein or carbs, for example, meat, fish or noodle to fill you for a longer period of time. 
After a vegetable soup you'll feel hungry soon, that's why we recommend to add something more nutritious to it or add it to snacks, not a meal.
